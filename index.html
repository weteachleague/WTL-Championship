0.0Introduction

The following rules are for the WTL Championship 2026 associated with We Teach League. By participating in the league, players are acknowledging that they have read and understand the rules in their entirety.

I’d like to say a big thank you to the people over at LBLCS. The rule book below has been highly influenced by their work. We think they are doing a great job, which is why their rule book was a great starting point for WTL events.

0.1Out of Game Behavior

0.1.1

Any harassment or personal attacks or any kind will result in penalties for the player(s) in question depending on the severity of the acts. This includes but is not limited to: bullying, racism, personal insults, and offensive language.

0.1.2

Disrespectfulness of any kind will not be tolerated.

0.1.3

Not safe for work content of any kind has no place in the discord.

0.1.4

Arguing with staff decisions will result in a warning. Inquiring about interpretation or implementation of a rule is allowed, as long as it is not in regards to a punishment that was handed down.

0.1.5

Team names, team logos, and player names must be appropriate. Players will be asked by admins to change a team name, a team logo, or player names if they are deemed inappropriate.

0.2In Game Behavior

0.2.1

Emotes that can be muted in-game (e.g., emote wheel, mastery flash, situational emotes for first blood) are allowed to be used. Dancing and laughing are also allowed. If bothered by these emotes, players can mute them through their settings (dancing and laughing are muted by muting chat).

0.2.2

All chat should only be used in a sportsmanlike manner and when warranted. E.g., “ggwp,” “glhf”. Mean-spirited all chat usage may result in game suspensions for the offending player(s).

0.2.3

If any situations come up during game time (eligibility questions, rule violations, unsportsmanlike behavior in the lobby, etc.), do not play the game. Captains should ping one of the officials on the discord server.

0.2.4

Whenever a new or reworked champion is released, they will not be available in league matches until 1 patch after the champion is released.

0.2.5

In order to participate in an official match for a team, players must be in the discord server and be properly registered on that team. The onus is on the opposing captain to check that the players in their lobby are registered on the approved roster lists.

0.2.6

The matches will take place every Friday at 8pm EST for NA and 7pm CET for EU. The captains are free to schedule their matches at another time, but it must be approved by both captains. Teams must be willing to field substitute players if need be.

0.2.7

Matches must be played 5 vs. 5. If the initial 5 players for each team are not present at the scheduled match time, see the table below:

Time late to matchPunishment
5 minutesLoss of 1st ban
10 minutesLoss of 1st two bans
20 minutesLoss of 1st game
30 minutesLoss of whole match/series

Please note that captains are encouraged to enforce these punishments to incentivise respect and sportsmanship, but not obligated to do so.

1.0Eligibility & Player Ratings

1.1Player Eligibility

1.1.1

All players must play on the account that is listed as the player’s main account on the roster sheet (unless given permission otherwise).

1.1.2

Players must report all accounts that are level 30 or higher.

1.2Player Ratings

1.1.2

Evaluation will be made with our SLOPE system (Standardized Level Of Play Estimation) which is an estimation calculated with many variables including Current rank, Peak rank, Role, amount of games played, champions played, etc… See this file for in depth explanations: SLOPE.pdf

The Code of the formula is in this one if you’re interested: SLOPE_Calc.ipynb

Finally, here is a website with all the informations of the tournament including a SLOPE calculator so it’s easy for everyone to calculate their own rating as well as an option to get the full team rating: SLOPE Calculator.

Point values by division (each tier division covers 0–99 LP; Master brackets are total LP):

1.2.1

All players will receive a numerical point rating according to the table above. Players may climb throughout the championship without it affecting their point value (unless it is clear that a player was rank-sitting, in which case staff has the discretion to adjust point values upwards).

1.2.2

There will be no climbing caps for the duration of the Championship.

1.2.3

If we find any player to be manipulating their evaluation in any way (rank sitting, playing on hidden account…), the offending player will be banned from the league and the captain will receive a formal warning or be refused entry in the league altogether.

1.2.4

Each team may have up to one player below the point bracket who will be worth 1 point. (This is to accommodate any team that is already made prior to the event, but isn’t recommended if you are building a team for the event.)

2.0Divisions

2.1Accepted Teams

2.1.1

All teams must consist of at least 5 players. Teams may have a maximum of 10 player slots. A player registered twice (for 2 different roles) will take 2 slots.

2.1.2

The Championship will accept at most 8 total teams per division.

2.2Evaluating Teams’ Point Evaluations

2.2.1

All accepted players will be assigned a point value based on the stipulations outlined in section 1.2.

2.2.2

A team’s point evaluation (TPE) is determined by adding the assigned point values of the 5 starters on the roster, known for the rest of this document as TPE players.

2.2.3

A player cannot change his role at any point post-registration. However, a player can be registered twice in a team (starter Top, substitute ADC for example) and will receive 2 SLOPE evaluations.

2.3Teams’ Point Evaluation Limits

2.3.1

TPE limits are as follows:

DivisionTPE range
NA · Division 128–38 points
NA · Division 210–26 points
NA · Division 328–38 points
EU · Division 120–35 points
EU · Division 228–38 points
2.3.1.1

This is a soft cap and the league holds the right to accept or deny any team with the goal to have as many teams competing while holding our standards regarding competitive integrity.

2.3.2

Substitutes must be at or below the point evaluation of their starting player.

2.3.3

4 out of the 5 players of each team must be a member of the WTL academies. The remaining one may be a non-member. This rule will keep being applied for the full duration of the event.

2.3.3.1

If a team uses subs, this rule stays the same. For each game played, at least 4 players must be a WTL member.

3.0Roster Changes

3.1Mid-Match Substitutions and Roster Announcements

3.1.1

Any substitute on a team’s roster may play in any game, but the team may be given a ban penalty (see [3.1.2.3]).

3.1.2

If a team will not play with its 5 starters, the captain should post the match roster in the roster announcement channel at least 24 hours in advance. If the team plays with its 5 starters, no announcement is needed.

3.1.2.1

If a team plans to make substitutions during a match, each sub must be on the announced roster, and the announcement in [3.1.2] must say which games each sub will play (see [3.1.2.3]).

3.1.2.2

The announced roster must have between 5 and 7 players.

3.1.2.3

A team may still change its roster at the last minute, but it must give the other team 10 extra minutes to prepare, and it loses two bans in the first ban phase of every game where its roster differs from the one announced 24 hours before the game.

3.2Other Roster Stipulations

3.2.1

If a player is removed from a team for breaking the rules or for any other reason, the team has 1 week from the league’s announcement of the ruling to fill that spot on its roster. The new player must have an equal or lower point value.

3.2.2

A player can be a starter on only 1 roster at a time. They may also be a sub on another team, as long as that team is in a different division.

4.0Pauses, Disconnects, Remakes, Forfeits

4.1Pauses and Breaks

4.1.1

If a player disconnects, or if any serious issue occurs, the game can be paused for up to 10 minutes by each team per individual game. If the pause surpasses 10 total minutes of pause time from a singular team, the game must unpause and be played out or the offending team must forfeit.

4.1.2

Teams may not pause during a fight or right before a fight will begin. Both captains should confirm they are ready before the game is unpaused.

4.1.2.1

A “fight” is defined as a sequence in the game where at least 2 players are in active combat with one another. The phrase “before a fight” refers to an instance where it is clear that a fight will break out within a few seconds (ex: A jungler starting a gank or a scuttle crab contest).

4.1.3

For games after the first game of a series, all players should be in the lobby and the draft should start no later than 15 minutes following the conclusion of the previous game. Games should start no more than 5 minutes after the draft finishes.

4.2Disconnects

4.2.1

If teams run into unfixable problems, such as a power or internet outage, the captain should contact the other captain immediately. If the issue is deemed fixable, the game will not be remade or postponed. The /pause function is approved for use during these situations. If the other captain is in agreement, teams may surpass their originally allotted 10 minutes of pause time.

4.3Remakes

4.3.1

Unless both parties are experiencing massive server lag, remakes will not be given. Natural disasters or life-threatening situations are the only other instances where a remake may be considered. Decisions will be made by the admin team once they understand the situation.

4.3.2

Remakes are not permitted if a player incorrectly selects their runes or summoner spells.

5.0Format of Play

5.1Voice Channels

5.1.1

All registered players must be in the Discord server with the proper naming convention.

5.1.2

Teams must play in their designated Discord voice channel for all matches. The voice channels are locked to just players.

5.2Format of Games

5.2.1

All matches should be played in custom lobbies with the abbreviations as the name and “WTL123” as the password. (ex: WD vs MRS, pw:WTL123)

5.2.2

All matches are played in a best-of-3 format, except for finals, which will be a best-of-5.

5.2.4

All the lobbies must be open to any player, coach, manager or team owner that wishes to spectate the match.

5.2.5

Any member of the league may stream and cast the game on twitch, but must give their twitch link in the appropriate channel in the discord server.

5.3Draft

5.3.1

All matches will use the “Fearless” draft format and must be done in Drafter.lol.

5.3.1.1

After the draft is completed, each team must specify the role of each champion during the role confirmation phase (in Drafter.lol).

5.3.2

In the game lobby, all players must line up in the standard order that follows the roles they intend to play before starting the draft.

  1. Top
  2. Jungle
  3. Mid
  4. Bottom
  5. Support

5.4Side Selection

5.4.1

First selection is chosen through a coin flip. Both captains shall go in the coin flip channel where one captain will flip a coin on this website or simply on google. The captain that won the coin flip makes the choice described in [5.4.1.1]. All games after the first are the loser's choice: the team that lost the most recent game makes that choice for the next game.

5.4.1.1

For the first selection, the team with the choice (the coin flip winner in game 1, then the loser of the previous game) gets to choose either 1st/2nd pick or blue/red side. The other team gets to make the choice that wasn’t made.

5.4.2

In semi-finals, first selection is chosen by coin flip as well (see [5.4.1]), but the 1st seed of the Swiss stage will have automatic first selection as well as opponent choice.

5.4.3

For finals, first selection is decided by a coin flip (see [5.4.1])

5.4.4

In the playoffs, the team with first selection of the first game must communicate their decision at least 24 hours prior to the start of the match with their roster. If this team does not decide on a side by this time, the first selection will be decided like in regular season with a coin flip.

5.5Schedules

5.5.1

Every week, each team will have 1 match scheduled.

5.5.2

These matches must be played within the week they are assigned (Between Monday and Sunday).

5.5.3

Weekly matchups are distributed prior to the championship starting.

5.5.4

The default time will be Friday 8 pm EST for NA and Friday 7pm CET for EU

5.5.4.1

If both captains can find a time that works better for them, they are free to change the date and time of their game as long as it’s in the same week. If an agreement can’t be reached, the game has to be played at the default time.

5.5.5

Finals have to be played at a preset time (more info coming soon)

5.6Tie Breakers

5.6.1

These tie breakers apply to any tie in the standings. Here are the tie breakers in order of priority:

  1. Match wins: Series record.
  2. Game difference: games won minus games lost across all Bo3s.
  3. Head-to-head: who won the series between the tied teams, if they played each other.
  4. Opponents’ total wins: teams that faced tougher opponents rank higher.
  5. Top Lane Death Match: Both top laners have to 1 v 1 in a skill matchup of their choice (both have to agree) on summoner’s rift. If an OTP wants his champion no matter what, he may declare it, but the other player gets counterpick choice. First one who gets a kill, first tower, or 100 CS wins the game. Win the 1 v 1 twice to win the tie breaker (Bo3).
  6. If there’s a 3 way tie after the first 4 tie breakers, there will be 1 v 1s between the top laners of all 3 teams with the following format (all Bo1s):
    • Game 1: Player 1 vs Player 2
    • Game 2: Player 3 vs Loser of Game 1
    • Game 3: Winner of Game 1 vs Winner of game 2

6.0Championship Format

6.1Swiss Stage

6.1.1

The initial matchups are decided with a draw.

6.1.2

After the first week, each team will be placed in a bracket according the the results of the precedent week (1-0 bracket and 0-1 bracket).

6.1.3

This process will be repeated an additional time for 3 total matches.

6.1.4

After those 3 matches, the top 4 seeds will move on to the semi-finals, and the 1st seed will get first selection and opponent choice for semi-finals. Ties are broken with [5.6].

6.1.5

In a division with 7 teams, one team has a bye each week: in week 1 it is decided by the draw, in week 2 it goes to a team at 0-1 and in week 3 to the team at 0-2. No team can get more than one bye. A bye counts as a series win for the team’s record, but adds 0 to its game difference (see [5.6]).

6.2Semi-Finals

6.2.1

The 1st seed will get to choose their opponent and the other 2 teams will play against each other. (see [5.4.2])

6.2.2

The 1st seed will have to let the other team know who their opponent choice is by Monday 8pm (EST/CET) of the semi-finals week at the latest and their first selection 24 hours in advance.

6.3Finals

6.3.1

Finals will be played in a Best-of-5 format between the 2 winning teams of semi-finals.

6.3.2

If both teams agree, they may play a Best-of-3 instead, but if one of the teams wishes to play a Best-of-5, the other team must comply.

May the best team reap the glory and the rewards, enjoy the event!

Section B

SLOPE Evaluations

The Standardized Level Of Play Estimate, by WD Maverick. One formula that applies to everyone and gives a fair picture of your level of play in a competitive setting.

Variables

x
Current rank, at the time the application is submitted
y
Peak rank in 2026 (used with at least 200 games in 2026)
y′
Peak rank across 2025 and 2026 (used with fewer than 200 games)
z
Ranked solo/duo games played in 2026
a
OTP status: games played on your most played champion in 2026 (u = a / z is your share of games on it)
b
Role status: 0 on-role, ½ secondary role, 1 off-role

How it reads

Only ranked solo/duo games count. Ranks use the apex-tier scale with Master 0 LP as the zero point, so Diamond 4 0 LP is −400 LP, Emerald 4 0 LP is −800 LP, and so on. Apex players who decayed recently use their rank from before the decay.

Your current rank is the base. It is pulled toward your peak by a weight of 150/z + ¼, so the more games you play, the more the result leans toward your current rank. At 600 games the result sits halfway between peak and current. The peak never fully disappears: the weight never drops below a quarter.

The penalty terms account for one-trick players and players off their main role. q scales from 0 at 50% on one champion to 1 at 100%; below 50% there is no OTP adjustment. The qb term in Division 1 covers players who are both one-tricks and off-role.

With fewer than 200 games it is hard to estimate your level, so your SLOPE starts from your highest peak of the last two seasons instead of your current rank. The one-trick and role adjustments still apply.

q(a) = 2a4(k−1)a + 3 − 2k − 1,   k = ln 0.7 / ln 0.75
if a < 0.50 → q = 0  ·  if z < 200 → f = y′ − 300q − 400b − 300qb
